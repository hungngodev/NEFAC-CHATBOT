 AN ACT RELATING TO PUBLIC RECORDS — ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS

 

 Excludes preliminary drafts, notes, impressions, memoranda, working papers and work products involving research at state institutions of higher learning from the definition of a public record.

 AN ACT RELATING TO ELECTIONS — PUBLIC RECORDS —RESTORATION OF VOTING RIGHTS

 

 Requires the department of corrections to provide certain nonprofit organizations with a list containing information about formerly incarcerated inmates who have become eligible to vote to assist in their efforts in registering former felons to vote.

 Filippi, Quattrocchi, Roberts, Lancia, Chippendale

 

 AN ACT RELATING TO STATE AFFAIRS AND GOVERNMENT — AUDIO AND VIDEO RECORDINGS

 

 Requires audio and video recordings of all open meetings of state and quasi-public agencies and bodies be transmitted to the secretary of state’s office for posting on its website to be available for public access.

 

5512

 

 Mendonca, Roberts, Filippi, Quattrocchi, Lombardi

 

 AN ACT RELATING TO STATE AFFAIRS AND GOVERNMENT — OPEN MEETINGS

 

 Requires the attorney general to, within ninety (90) days of a complaint alleging a violation of the open meetings law, file a report detailing the results of the investigation into that complaint. The report would be deemed a public record.

 AN ACT RELATING TO STATE AFFAIRS AND GOVERNMENT — OPEN MEETINGS

 

 Allows school committees to add item(s) to their agenda for the purposes of discussion only without having to publish notice of the additional item(s).

5732

 

 Canario, Fellela, Azzinaro, Bennett, Johnston

 

 AN ACT RELATING TO STATE AFFAIRS AND GOVERNMENT — OPEN MEETINGS

 

 Requires that all public bodies keep official minutes of all meetings and file copies of these minutes with the secretary of state within thirty-five (35) days of the meeting for inspection by the public.

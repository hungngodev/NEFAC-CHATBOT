NEFAC created knownewengland.org in 2011 to give the public insight into the value of public records and the type of information that can be accessed through various FOI laws. The data provided on this site — related to Education, Fiscal Priorities, Public Safety and Environment — is all accessible through public documents. Please visit knownewengland.org and learn more about how you can best use your state’s FOI law and what type of information is available to you.
